Who Were the Signers

Famous Signers

Some of the most famous signers of the declaration of Independence include

Thomas Jefferson-
The principal author of the Declaration and later the 3rd President of the United States.
John Adams-
Major advocate for independence and later the 2nd President of the United States.
Benjamin Franklin-
Famous scientist, inventor, diplomat, and statesman; he was 70 when he signed.
John Hancock-
President of the Continental Congress; famous for his very large signature. He was the first person to sign.
Samuel Adams-
Revolutionary leader and cousin of John Adams; later governor of Massachusetts.
John Witherspoon-
A minister and educator who was the only active college president to sign the Declaration.
Charles Carroll of Carrollton-
One of the wealthiest men in the colonies and the last surviving signer, dying in 1832.
Roger Sherman-
One of the few people to sign the Declaration of Independence, Articles of Confederation, and U.S. Constitution.
