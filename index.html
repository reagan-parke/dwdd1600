Digital Citizenship

What is Digital Citizenship?

Digital citizenship is the responsible, safe, and respectful use of technology and the internet.

What is Digital Law?

Digital law refers to the laws and rules that govern how people use technology, the internet, and digital content.

What is AUP?

AUP stands for Acceptable Use Policy. It is a set of rules that explains how people are allowed to use technology, networks, devices, or the internet.

What is Plagerism?

Plagiarism is when someone uses another person's work, ideas, or words and pretends that they are their own without giving the original person credit.

What is Academic Dishonesty?

Academic dishonesty is any form of cheating or being dishonest in schoolwork or academic activities.

What are the rules for AI Genegrated Content?

Use AI honestly, responsibly, and according to the rules, and make sure the final work represents your own learning.
