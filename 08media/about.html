About Us

Our Mission

Our mission is to help people understand the hidden language of art. We believe that art can communicate things that words sometimes cannot. A color can create a feeling, a small symbol can tell a story, and the size of an object can change the way we understand it. Even the materials an artist chooses can give a piece meaning. We want to help people understand the choices artists make and how those choices can change the way we experience and understand a piece of art.
